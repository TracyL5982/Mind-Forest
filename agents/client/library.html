Every way the Renderer can draw one card (components) and every way it can arrange several sibling cards (composites). This page draws them with the same code and styles as the live client, from sample data.

Solid cards are yours. Dashed amber cards are picks the librarian brought and you have not kept.

Under each sample is its type name and its footprint in pixels, which is worked out from the content and never measured.

The first eleven types are drawn from an item's kind and its words. The fourteen after them are drawn from structure the item carries, such as its facts, its rows, its entries or a catalogue's record, or from a passage of its words. The last, a sheet, is assembled from parts of those kinds when no one card covers the item. Each is shown from the sample the service gives it, then with other content, in Chinese, and at its extremes, some of them opened.

Any card can carry one line of chips under its content: a date, a place, people and, for what the agents brought, where it came from. A date is always whole, and a longer place, name or source is clipped inside its chip. Each type is shown once without chips and once with them. The line adds 28 pixels and nothing else changes.
